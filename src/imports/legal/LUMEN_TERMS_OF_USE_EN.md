## 5. ALGORITHMIC ANALYSIS AND POLITICAL-PHILOSOPHICAL PROFILE DIAGNOSIS

**5.1 Nature of automated analysis.** Lumen offers **automated algorithmic analysis** features based exclusively on the reading history recorded by the user within the Platform:

- **Political-philosophical profile diagnosis:** the system processes works marked as read by the user and deterministically calculates positions across **9 analytical axes** — (i) Freedom vs. Authority; (ii) State vs. Market vs. Community; (iii) Tradition vs. Rupture; (iv) Individual vs. Collective; (v) Epistemology (Rationalist vs. Empiricist); (vi) Ontology (Idealist vs. Materialist vs. Interactionist); (vii) Historicity (Ahistorical vs. Historicist vs. Dialectical); (viii) Philosophical Anthropology (Optimist vs. Pessimist vs. Ambivalent); and (ix) Scope of Application (Universalist vs. Particularist) — generating an **SVG radar chart** and an automated **textual diagnosis**.
- **Intelligent recommendation system:** the algorithm identifies imbalances (*gaps*) in the user's reading profile — defined as a concentration exceeding 55% or 65% on one pole of any analytical axis — and suggests works and thinkers that could complement and diversify the user's reading repertoire.

**5.2 Educational and non-determinative character.** The diagnosis generated by the Platform constitutes an **educational synthesis** constructed from the works selected by the user and the taxonomic metadata defined by TechMinds' editorial curation. This diagnosis does **not constitute** a scientific, psychological, political, sociological, or professional assessment of any kind. The output reflects solely the user's reading choices within the Platform and does not necessarily represent the user's personal beliefs, political affiliations, or worldview.

**5.3 No legally significant external effects.** The algorithmic analysis implemented does not produce decisions with legal effects or decisions that significantly affect the user's rights or interests outside the Platform environment. Accordingly, it is not subject to the restrictions governing automated decision-making under **Article 20 of the LGPD**, **Article 22 of the GDPR**, or analogous provisions. The user may, at any time, fully clear their reading history through the functionality available in the interface and restart the diagnostic process from scratch.

**5.4 Algorithmic transparency.** The logic underlying the profile diagnosis and the recommendation system is entirely based on **deterministic, transparent rules**, without the use of machine learning models, neural networks, generative artificial intelligence, or any form of opaque statistical processing. The scoring criteria, analytical axes, and gap thresholds are described in the Platform interface and in these Terms.
